# Coffee Chat Questions

Universal + role-specific + trap questions for coffee chats and informational interviews.

> Rule: personalize before using. Never open with a template question. Warm up with something from their LinkedIn or a recent post first.

---

## Universal (always work)

- What does a typical week look like for you?
- What do you wish you had known before joining [firm]?
- What skills matter most in your role that maybe surprised you?
- How did the MBA prepare you, and where did you have to fill gaps yourself?
- What is your honest take on the culture? What makes someone thrive here vs struggle?

Closing every chat:
- Is there anyone else you would suggest I speak with?
- Would it be okay if I kept you updated as I go through the process?

---

## Trap questions (filter for role fit)

These are the questions Thaiz should ALWAYS ask when a Tier 1 firm is on the table. The answers separate the strategic version of a role from the operational version. If the answer to a trap question sounds bad, downgrade the firm on your list even if the brand name is prestigious.

### Innovation / NPD / Commercialization

**Trap:** in some CPGs "Innovation MBA" is really R&D coordination and lab logistics, not strategy. You want the seat where you shape the what and why of new products, not the seat that manages sample shipments.

**Ask:**

1. In your team, what percentage of an Innovation MBA's time is strategic (defining what to build and why) vs operational (coordinating R&D, samples, timelines)?
2. Can you walk me through the last new product your team launched? Who owned the concept, who owned the PnL, who owned the go to market?
3. What KPIs does an Innovation MBA get evaluated on in their first year? (Good answer: new revenue targets, margin vs plan, project count with clear scope. Bad answer: only project execution or generic team support.)
4. How much time does an Innovation MBA spend with the brand teams vs inside R&D vs with commercial? What is the ideal split?
5. When you look at Innovation MBAs who have thrived here, what did they do differently in year one?

### Brand Management (ABM / BM)

**Trap:** in some CPGs the first two years of ABM are deck making and managing agencies, with real PnL ownership only arriving at year three or later. You want the seat where you get PnL early.

**Ask:**

1. How quickly does an MBA hire typically get PnL ownership of a brand or subrand? First year, second, third?
2. In the first six months, what does the ABM actually do day to day? What percentage is analytics, agency management, presentation building, vs strategic brand work?
3. What is the ratio of MBAs who convert from internship to full time, and of those, how many are still in Brand at year three?
4. What separates the ABMs who become brand builders from the ones who become executors?
5. How much say does an ABM have on brand strategy vs executing what the BM decided?

### Growth (only if forced to look, deprioritized)

**Trap:** growth is highly operational at most CPGs. Skip if pattern feels off.

**Ask:**

1. How much of the growth role is upstream strategy vs downstream execution (media buys, channel ops)?
2. Where does growth sit in the org, and who does it report to?

## Consulting specific (still Plan Z)

- How do you describe the CPG / consumer practice to someone evaluating it?
- How much Latin America exposure do associates get early on?
- What is the case interview process at [firm] specifically, any nuances I should know?

---

## Positioning yourself in coffee chats

**One line intro:** *"I'm an incoming Booth MBA from Peru with an engineering + PM background. I built financial products for base of the pyramid consumers at BCP and Favo, and now I am moving into brand and product roles at large CPG."*

**When they ask why CPG:** *"I want to learn how to create products and services inside an established company where the best practices already exist. I want to see how mature orgs build brands with emotional resonance at scale."*

**When they ask Innovation or Brand:** *"Both, honestly. I want summer 2027 to be the tiebreaker. My engineer + PM combo leans me toward Innovation, but the classic BM path is also strong for the leadership I want to grow into."*

---

## After the chat

Send thank you within 24 hours. Reference one specific thing they said (not a generic "great conversation"). Within one week, send them something useful (an article, a warm intro, a piece of your own thinking). At day 60, close the loop with progress: "since we talked I've done X, and your framing on Y shaped this."
