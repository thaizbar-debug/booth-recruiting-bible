# Networking Tracker

Track every coffee chat, cold outreach, and relationship. This is your recruiting CRM.

## How to Use This

Add a row for every person you reach out to. Update status as you progress. Review weekly — anyone "Followed Up" for 3+ weeks without response should get a second touch.
