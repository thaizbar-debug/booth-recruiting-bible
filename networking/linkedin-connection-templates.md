# LinkedIn Connection Request Templates

Plantillas reusables de 300 caracteres para connection requests.
Regla fija: **sin guiones (em/en dashes)** en ningún mensaje.

---

## Track-First (Pivoter)

**Cuándo usar:** cold puro, sin connection warm. La persona ya está en el rol/track que quieres y tú lideras con tu objetivo de carrera + nota de humildad.

```
Hi [NAME], I'm an incoming Booth MBA '28 going after brand and product roles that shape consumer experience in CPG, wellness, and retail. I know it's a competitive path and I'd love to hear how you got there. Open to a 20 minute chat?
```

~225 / 300 caracteres.

### Variables a swapear

- **`[NAME]`** — primer nombre
- **Functional focus** — actualmente "brand and product roles that shape consumer experience." Si quieres focalizar más, cambia por: "brand management at CPG companies," "product roles in wellness," "strategy roles at consumer brands," etc.
- **Industries** — actualmente "CPG, wellness, and retail." Ajusta según la persona target.

---

## Connection-First

**Cuándo usar:** tienes una conexión warm que abre la puerta (programa compartido, alumni, Latina network, brand user, etc.). Lideras con esa conexión, no con tu pitch.

```
Hi [NAME]! I'm Thaiz, incoming Booth MBA from Peru and [CONNECTION HOOK]. Would love to connect and hear [WHAT YOU WANT TO LEARN]. Any chance you'd have 15 minutes for a virtual coffee in the coming weeks? Thank you!
```

### Variables a swapear

**`[CONNECTION HOOK]`** — el por qué de esta persona específica:
- "part of [Program Name] this summer" (Inspire, MLT, ROMBA)
- "exploring [industry/firm] this fall"
- "fellow Latina in the [firm] orbit"
- "a longtime user of your brand"
- "fascinated by your work at [Company]"

**`[WHAT YOU WANT TO LEARN]`** — específico, no genérico:
- "how you think about the [office/practice/team]"
- "how you ended up at [Company]"
- "what drew you to [specific area]"
- "your perspective on [function] at [Company]"

### Ejemplo lleno (Honor, McKinsey Inspire)

> Hi Honor! I'm Thaiz, incoming Booth MBA from Peru and part of McKinsey Inspire this summer. Would love to connect and hear how you think about the Bay Area office. Any chance you'd have 15 minutes for a virtual coffee in the coming weeks? Thank you!

244 / 300 caracteres.

## Decision tree rápido

| Situación | Template |
|---|---|
| Tienes connection warm (programa, alumni, mutual, eres usuaria del brand) | Connection-First |
| Cold puro a alguien ya en el rol que quieres | Track-First |
| Founder de startup pequeña (CPG / wellness) | Ir al formato largo de `startup-outreach-messages.md` |
