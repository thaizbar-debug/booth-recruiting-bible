# MBA Programs by Tier 1 Company

Reference for summer 2027 recruiting. Confirmed 2026-07-08.

**Filter rule:** large established CPG + formal MBA program + food / wellness / beverage core.

**Function target:** Innovation / NPD / Commercialization OR Brand Management. Growth rejected as too operational.

> Program names change year to year. Confirm each on the company career page before writing applications.

## Timing calendar

Approximate for CPG (different from consulting):

| Window | Milestone |
|---|---|
| August 2026 (Booth Week 1) | Company info sessions begin. Attend every Tier 1. |
| September to October 2026 | Coffee chats with alumni recruiters. Warm outreach point of maximum leverage. |
| November to December 2026 | Applications open and close. Many CPG close in November, earlier than consulting. |
| January to February 2027 | Interviews (behavioral + brand challenge + light case). |
| March 2027 | Offers. |

**Key difference from consulting:** CPG interviews are behavioral + brand challenge (they give you a product and ask for a strategy), not classic case interview. Start brand case prep in October 2026.
