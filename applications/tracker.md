## CPT & Visa Notes

- CPT requires enrollment + academic connection (internship tied to Booth curriculum)
- Booth Career Services coordinates CPT authorization — meet them Week 1 of Year 1
- Large firms (MBB, P&G, Unilever) have international student processes — CPT is not unusual
- Avoid small startups without established international hiring processes
- **File CPT paperwork early** — international affairs processing takes weeks
- Lead with your value prop; visa is an administrative detail, not a dealbreaker
