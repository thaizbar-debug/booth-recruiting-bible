# LAMP List — Master Employer Tracker

The LAMP list is your 40+ employer brainstorm sorted by what actually matters: **Motivation first, then Postings, then Alumni**. Your top 6 by alumni access are who you work first.

> **The rule:** You don't apply from the LAMP list — you network from it. Applications come after conversations.

## How to Build Your LAMP List

Four brainstorm sources — use all four:
1. **LinkedIn Alumni Tool** — Booth alumni at target firms (`linkedin.com/school/university-of-chicago-booth-school-of-business/people/`)
2. **Job Postings** — Search your target role keywords; note which companies post most frequently
3. **Dream Companies** — Places you'd work regardless of postings (prestige, mission, brand)
4. **Relevant companies** — Companies that come up repeatedly in your research, industry press, etc.

**LinkedIn Groups Hack:** Add `&network=%5B%22F%22%5D` to any LinkedIn search URL to filter for 2nd-degree connections only — surfaces warm-intro paths you'd otherwise miss.

## Sorting Logic

| Column | What It Means | Sort Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **L — List** | Source: LinkedIn/Dream/Posting/Relevant | — |
| **A — Alumni** | # of Booth alumni you can find there | **#1 — highest first** |
| **M — Motivation** | How much you want to work there (1–5) | **#2 — highest first** |
| **P — Postings** | # of relevant open roles right now | **#3 — highest first** |

Sort by **A desc, then M desc, then P desc**. Your top 6 after sorting = your first outreach wave.

## LAMP Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not Started** | On the list, haven't researched yet |
| **Researching** | Reading about the company, finding contacts |
| **Outreach Sent** | 6-Point Email sent, waiting for response |
| **3-Day Check** | 3 business days passed — try different contact at same firm |
| **7-Day Follow-Up** | 7 business days — send follow-up via different channel |
| **Chat Scheduled** | Informational meeting booked |
| **Chat Complete** | TIARA done, follow-up sent |
| **In Relationship** | Monthly Ben Franklin check-ins active |
| **Applied** | Formal application submitted |
| **Inactive** | Deprioritized — no alumni access or low motivation |
