## Fit with the Company

**Why this company?**
→ Prepare specific: (1) A brand or business unit you want to work on and why. (2) A strategic challenge you find compelling. (3) Something you learned from a Booth alum who works there. (4) LATAM angle — "I see a significant untapped opportunity in [market] and I want to be part of the team that pursues it."

**What are the major challenges for the company? What can be improved?**
→ Research-based. Use Yahoo Finance, SmartBrief, and the company's annual report. Frame challenges as opportunities: "I see [X] as a challenge — here's how I'd think about addressing it."

**What are the company growth engines?**
→ Prepare per company. General frame for CPGs: premium tier growth, international expansion (especially LATAM), digital/D2C channels, innovation in adjacencies.

**Where do you see the company going in the next 5-10 years?**
→ Strategic view, specific, with your LATAM / emerging market lens as a differentiator.

## Fit with General Management Role

**Why are you pursuing a GM role?**
→ "General Management is where the full business comes together — P&L, people, strategy, and execution. I've been operating as a de facto GM in my PM roles: owning the product roadmap, managing cross-functional teams, reporting to C-suite. A formal GM track gives me the breadth and visibility I need to eventually lead a business unit."

**What skills do you have that would make you a good general manager?**
→ Three: (1) Structural thinking — I build frameworks that make complex organizations simpler. (2) Empathetic leadership — I bring people along, especially in crisis. (3) Analytical rigor — I use data to make and defend decisions.

**Have you had direct supervisory experience?**
→ Yes. At BCP, led a product team with engineers, designers, and analysts — all senior to me in tenure. At Favo, managed PMs and coordinated across the Brazil and Peru teams. "My supervisory style is: clear expectations, regular 1:1s, and honest feedback in both directions."

**What is your leadership or management style?**
→ "Clarity-first. I spend more time than most making sure the team agrees on the problem before we discuss solutions. That sounds obvious, but most misalignment comes from people solving different problems in parallel. Once we're aligned on the diagnosis, I give people ownership and hold them to outcomes, not process."

## Behavioral Questions

**Describe a team experience that did not work well.**
→ Favo / Brazil misalignment. "Two teams building incompatible features because I assumed alignment without verifying it. Learned to close the loop explicitly and verify shared understanding at every milestone."

**Describe a situation with ambiguity.**
→ Favo post-Brazil shutdown. No clear mandate, 300 people laid off, leadership vacuum. Created structure: unified strategy committee, clear priorities, governance.

**Describe a situation where you had to convince a team or manager about an idea.**
→ BCP auction system. Piloted it to avoid the debate becoming theoretical.

**Tell me about a time you had to persuade someone resistant to act.**
→ Apoyo client (family business owner). Reframed the recommendation from "fixing the business" to "protecting your legacy." Same recommendation, completely different reception.

**Tell me about a time you had to make a decision without enough information.**
→ Favo triage decision. 72 hours, incomplete data, high stakes. Built a rapid framework with available signals.

**Tell me about a time you had a disagreement with your manager.**
→ Be specific. Frame: I raised the disagreement directly and privately first, presented my reasoning with data, respected their final decision while making my position known.

**Tell me about a time you gave feedback.**
→ During Favo restructuring — giving honest feedback to team members whose roles were changing or ending. "I believe hard truths delivered with care are a form of respect."

**Tell me about a time you received negative feedback.**
→ Consulting role — early feedback that my analysis was sometimes too deep for the client's decision timeline. Took it seriously, built a new habit of starting with "what decision does this need to support?"

**Tell me about a time you worked on a cross-functional project.**
→ BCP Warda — tech, business, data, design, and compliance. Or Apoyo — client-facing with back-office analysis in parallel.
