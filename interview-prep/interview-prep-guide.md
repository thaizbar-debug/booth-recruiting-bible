# How to Prepare for an Interview — Booth Framework

Source: Booth Career Services "Preparing for an Interview" guide

---

## The Core Framework: Why You? Why Now? Why Them?

Every interview answer, at some level, needs to touch all three:
- **Why you?** → Your experiences, skills, and what makes you uniquely valuable
- **Why them?** → Research the organization, know 3+ specific reasons you want to work there
- **Why now?** → Why this role fits your career path at this moment, and how it connects to your long-term goals

---

## 3 Steps to Prepare

### Step 1: Anticipate

1. Thoroughly review the job description — highlight every skill/experience they mention
2. Review the function-specific FAQ (see other files in this folder)
3. Consult 2nd-year Booth students who interned at your target firms
4. Research the company deeply (see resources/company-research.md)
5. Think like the interviewer: "What are the weaknesses in my candidacy for this specific role?"

**For Thaiz specifically — anticipate these potential objections:**
- "No US work experience" → Address with: LATAM is a priority market; Booth gives me the US context I'm adding
- "Career pivot from PM to consulting" → Address with: deliberate, not desperate; 50% pay cut proves conviction
- "International student / visa" → Address with: firms have CPT processes; lead with value, address proactively but briefly
- "No prior consulting-firm experience" → Address with: Apoyo is consulting; 15 projects; $15MM EBITDA work

### Step 2: Plan Your Responses

1. Write out responses to every question in your track's FAQ file (do this — don't skip it)
2. Identify which of your 7 STAR stories best maps to each question type (see story map in each track file)
3. Practice until responses are concise — under 2 minutes for behavioral, under 3 minutes for complex case responses
4. Manage nervous behaviors — record yourself, watch it back

**SOAR format for behavioral questions:**
- **Situation** — set the scene briefly (20% of your time)
- **Obstacle** — what made this hard (20%)
- **Action** — what you did specifically (50%)
- **Result** — what happened, quantified when possible (10%)

Booth uses SOAR (not STAR — they add "Obstacle" to emphasize complexity).

### Step 3: Rehearse

1. Record yourself on video answering questions — watch it back
2. Practice with a peer or coffee chat contact who can give real feedback
3. Book a mock interview with Booth Career Services (boothworks@ChicagoBooth.edu, 312-464-8677)
4. Use the Elevator Pitch module in VMOCK (login with your original Booth email: tbarthel@chicagobooth.edu)

## Types of Questions and How to Handle Each

| Type | Examples | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Behavioral** | "Tell me about a time when..." | SOAR format. Have 7 stories ready; mix and match. |
| **Technical** | "How do the 3 financial statements connect?" | State your framework first, then work through it. Ask clarifying questions. |
| **Self-Assessment** | "What's your greatest weakness?" | Be real, show self-awareness, show you're working on it. |
| **Fit** | "Why do you want to work here?" | Research-specific. Name people, initiatives, products. |
| **Stress** | Rapid-fire questions; disinterested interviewer | Stay calm. They're testing how you handle discomfort. This is a data point, not a judgment. |
| **Case/Problem Solving** | "If we opened another hub, where should it be?" | Structure first. Take notes. Ask clarifying questions. Explain your reasoning out loud. |

## Company Research Checklist (Complete Before Every Interview)

- [ ] Read the company's most recent earnings call or annual report (5 minutes on key themes)
- [ ] Check Yahoo Finance for recent stock performance, news, analyst ratings
- [ ] Read SmartBrief or industry news for their sector (last 30 days)
- [ ] Know their 3 biggest brands / products — strengths and weaknesses
- [ ] Know their main competitors and how they differentiate
- [ ] Know at least one recent strategic move (acquisition, launch, partnership)
- [ ] Know their LATAM presence (this is your differentiator angle)
- [ ] Look up the interviewer on LinkedIn — find one genuine connection point
- [ ] Prepare 3 thoughtful questions for them (see general-questions.md)

## Day-Of Checklist

- [ ] Read the company's most recent news (morning of the interview)
- [ ] Review your story map and the 3 differentiators you want them to remember
- [ ] Have your resume printed (if in-person) or in front of you (if virtual)
- [ ] Know the names of the people interviewing you
- [ ] Arrive / log on 5 minutes early
- [ ] Bring 2 thoughtful questions per interviewer
- [ ] Send thank-you emails within 24 hours (brief, specific to something from the conversation)
