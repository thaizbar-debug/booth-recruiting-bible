## Your Positioning for Marketing

**Headline:** "Product manager turned marketer — I've built products from zero to launch for base-of-the-pyramid consumers, and I understand what makes people emotionally adopt something new. That's not just marketing theory — it's lived customer empathy."

**Three differentiators:**
1. You've done real go-to-market strategy, not just theoretical — Warda launch, Favo GTM for mom-and-pop stores
2. You understand consumer behavior at the bottom of the pyramid — an underserved and increasingly important market
3. Engineering + Business = you can own both the analytical (P&L, market sizing) and the emotional (brand positioning) sides of marketing

## Fit with Marketing / Brand Management

**Give me an example of a well-marketed brand/product.**
→ Prepare 2-3 examples. Depth matters more than breadth.
- *Duolingo:* Gamification of learning. Brilliant use of streaks and rewards — exactly what we did at BCP with Warda. Turns a habit-formation challenge into a daily ritual. Emotional engagement is the product.
- *Dove (Real Beauty):* Redefined what beauty brand advertising could be. Moved from product features to identity and self-worth — the category the brand wants to own. Consistent, credible, and differentiated.
- *Nubank (LATAM lens):* Disrupted traditional banking with radical simplicity and brand personality. Purple card as status symbol in Brazil, WhatsApp-native customer service. Built brand love in a category people historically hated.

Why well-marketed? → Each: clear target, consistent message, emotionally resonant, distinctiveness in the category.

**Give me an example of a poorly marketed brand/product.**
→ Have 1-2 ready. Frame constructively — what would YOU do?
- Any CPG brand that relies only on product features, not identity.
- "Gap's 2010 logo redesign — they abandoned a 20-year brand asset for a generic font with no consumer rationale, and reversed in one week under social media backlash. The failure was ignoring brand equity and consumer attachment."

**What is your favorite brand/product and why?**
→ Choose one you know deeply and can defend. Have an answer for "how would you improve it?" Suggested: a CPG brand you can connect to your consumer experience (e.g., Natura, Dove, a brand relevant to LATAM consumers).

**Give me an example of an innovative new brand/product.**
→ Prepare something recent. Options: Liquid Death (canned water with punk brand identity); Prime (Logan Paul — cultural marketing, not traditional); anything that used a non-traditional channel effectively.

**What trends are facing Company X's industry today?**
→ Prepare per company. General frames: (1) AI personalization of marketing. (2) Consumer distrust of brand inauthenticity — brand purpose must be backed by action. (3) LATAM market growth as CPG expansion priority. (4) Private label pressure on branded CPGs. (5) Gen Z rejecting traditional advertising.

**What would you do if your brand had lost market share over the last five years?**
→ MECE framework: (1) Diagnose — price, distribution, product, promotion, competitive dynamics. (2) Segment — where is market share being lost? Which consumer segment? (3) Act — is it a brand perception problem (→ campaign), a product problem (→ innovation), or a distribution problem (→ trade strategy)?

**How would you determine whether to introduce a line extension?**
→ Framework: (1) Does the extension grow the category or cannibalize the core? (2) Is there genuine consumer demand (research, not assumption)? (3) Can you execute at quality without diluting brand equity? (4) Does it strengthen the core brand's position? Example: Dove Men+Care — extended into male care without diluting the Dove brand identity.

## Behavioral Questions

**Give me an example of a time you had to deal with team conflict.**
→ Story: BCP — tech vs. business silos. Created the "auction system" that transformed adversarial dynamics into collaborative competition. Outcome: launched Warda in 10 months.

**Give me an example of a time you took initiative.**
→ Story: Favo restructuring. No one asked me to design the unified strategy committee. I created the structure because the organization needed it.

**Give me an example of a time you led a project from start to finish.**
→ Story: BCP Warda launch. Full lifecycle: discovery → design → pilot → launch. 2 years of prior failure, launched in 10 months.

**Tell me about a time you had to motivate/influence others.**
→ Story: COVID WhatsApp challenge. Started free indoor cycling classes for 100+ people during lockdown. Built two editions of a 30-day fitness challenge that maxed out WhatsApp groups. No formal authority — pure influence and enthusiasm.

**Tell me about a time you had to analyze/interpret data to make a decision.**
→ Story: BCP Warda. A/B tests on gamification mechanics, pilot data on engagement and asset growth ($15M → $25M trajectory), used to convince senior leadership to approve full launch.

**Tell me about a time you had to solve a problem with limited data.**
→ Story: Favo triage decision. 72 hours to decide which products/markets to preserve post-Brazil shutdown. Built a rapid framework on available retention and unit economics data.

**Tell me about a time you presented an idea and there was an objection.**
→ Story: BCP auction system. Tech leadership preferred the existing quarterly roadmap. Addressed objection by running a pilot — "let the data make the case."

**Tell me about a time you had to come up with a creative solution.**
→ Story: BCP planning redesign (auction system). Or COVID WhatsApp challenge (community solution in lockdown with no budget).

## Company Research Framework (before every marketing interview)

1. **Know their 3 biggest brands** — strength, weakness, what you'd improve
2. **Know a recent campaign** — effective or not, and why
3. **Know their category trends** — what's threatening their market share?
4. **Know their LATAM presence** — this is your differentiator angle
5. **Have a suggestion ready** — "If I were brand manager on X, I would..."

> Use SmartBrief (food/bev or retail), WWD, and the company's own press releases to prep.
