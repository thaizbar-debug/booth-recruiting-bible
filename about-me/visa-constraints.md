## CPT — What It Is and How to Get It

**CPT = Curricular Practical Training.** Allows F-1 students to work off-campus when the work is integral to their curriculum.

**Requirements:**
- Must be enrolled full-time at Booth
- Internship must be tied to academic curriculum (Booth Career Services coordinates this — it's standard practice)
- Must be authorized BEFORE starting work — do not work before authorization
- CPT authorization is per position/employer — each new role needs separate authorization

**Practical implications:**
- Part-time CPT (less than 20 hrs/week): Can do during academic year without affecting OPT
- Full-time CPT (20+ hrs/week): Affects OPT eligibility if done for 12+ cumulative months
- Summer internship = full-time CPT — keep cumulative under 12 months to preserve OPT

**Process:** Booth's International Affairs Office handles CPT authorization. Apply early — process takes 2–4 weeks.

## OPT — Post-Graduation

- Applies automatically after graduation if applied for correctly
- 12 months base OPT
- STEM OPT extension: +24 months if graduating with a STEM-designated program
  - **Action item:** Confirm with Booth whether Marketing + Strategic Management concentrations qualify, or if she needs to add a STEM-designated course
- OPT must start within 60 days of graduation; can start up to 60 days before graduation date on academic transcript

## H-1B — Long-Term

- Employer must file H-1B petition by **April 1** for **October 1** start
- If graduating May 2028, gap-bridge: OPT covers May 2028 → October 2029 if STEM OPT extension granted
- All major consulting firms (MBB, Deloitte, Accenture) and CPG companies (P&G, Unilever, Nestlé, etc.) have H-1B sponsorship programs — this is NOT a differentiating barrier at these firms
- Do NOT pursue small companies or boutiques that historically do not sponsor

## How to Address Visa in Interviews

**When to bring it up:** Only when directly asked, or in final-round conversations.

**What to say (consulting):**
> "I'll be on CPT for the summer and will have OPT/STEM OPT authorization post-graduation. I know [firm] has a strong track record of supporting international students through the H-1B process — I've spoken with several Booth alums there and they confirmed it's standard. I'm planning for that timeline and it won't be an issue."

**What to say (CPG):**
> "I'll be on CPT for the summer internship and will have OPT authorization starting at graduation. I understand [company] sponsors H-1B — I'm planning for that timeline and have been proactive about understanding the process."

**Key message:** Visa is an administrative detail, not a risk. Proactive = professional. Never bring it up apologetically.
