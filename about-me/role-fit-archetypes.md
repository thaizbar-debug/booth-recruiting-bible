# Role Fit: What I Actually Want

**Date captured:** June 25, 2026.
**Source:** Self reflection by Thaiz, in her own words, refined into role archetypes.
**Use this file:** Whenever filtering a company, role, or recruiting decision. If a role does not match at least 4 of the 6 patterns below, it is probably not the right fit.

## The pattern in my own words (original Spanish)

> "Quiero una posición ágil, donde yo cree el producto o marca, que me genere emociones positivas al usar el producto o servicio, que el área tenga buen ambiente y que mi jefe tenga un strong background y el equipo sea joven, que la empresa tenga distintas categorías, sabores, tamaños para sus productos o servicios, puede ser un producto nuevo dentro de una empresa grande o una empresa mediana. Me gusta ser owner de algo, tener autonomía, estar en la maduración del concepto, no ejecutar en la operación pero sí cranear el camino. Me gusta ver cómo algo crece y mejora con el tiempo, me gusta ver algo por lo que he trabajado en las calles, sentirlo visto y usado por otros, me gusta ver cómo otros lo disfrutan igual que yo."

## The 6 patterns extracted

1. **Concept architect, not operator.** I want to shape the *what* and *why*, not run the daily operational machine. "Cranear el camino" but not execute it.
2. **Portfolio brain.** I am drawn to brands with variants, line extensions, multiple categories, flavors, sizes. Not single SKU stories.
3. **Mid stage maturation.** Not zero to one (too messy). Not optimization of a 50 year old brand (too rigid). I want something that has product market fit and needs to grow and evolve.
4. **Tangible in the wild.** I need to see it on shelves, in hands, in cities. Physical or experiential, not pure software.
5. **Emotional resonance.** The product has to *feel* like something. Me as user, then others as users, then shared joy.
6. **Tribe.** Young team, strong leader with real background, good ambiente.

## Role archetypes that match

### Tier 1 (highest fit)

**Brand Manager / Associate Brand Manager at challenger CPG with portfolio.**
Mid size, founder led, multiple SKUs I can own. Companies to research:
- Olipop, Poppi, Liquid Death (beverage portfolios with flavors)
- Graza, Fishwife, Omsom, Fly By Jing (food, multiple SKUs)
- Magic Spoon, Three Wishes (cereal, flavor variants)
- Athletic Brewing, Recess, De Soi (non alcoholic, multiple lines)
- Hatch, Lalo, Frida (parenting brand portfolios)

**Innovation / New Product Development inside large CPG.**
The "producto nuevo dentro de una empresa grande" pattern. Specific groups:
- Coca Cola Venturing & Emerging Brands
- PepsiCo Hive (the small / emerging brand incubator)
- Mondelez SnackFutures
- General Mills 301 Inc / G Works
- Unilever Foundry
- Mars Edge and Mars Wrigley innovation arms

**Brand strategist at a brand creation studio.**
I would help mature concepts across multiple client portfolios. Different from IDEO, which is more design heavy. Studios that build brands:
- Red Antler (built Allbirds, Casper, Brooklinen)
- Pattern Brands (a venture studio that creates household brands)
- Gin Lane lineage / Sub Rosa / Lippincott
- Wolff Olins

### Tier 2 (strong fit, slightly different shape)

**Category or sub brand lead at large beauty or fashion with portfolio.**
- Sephora Collection (the in house brand)
- Estée Lauder incubator brands
- Rare Beauty, Fenty (newer challenger inside a large parent)
- Glossier (multiple product lines, currently in maturation phase)

**Consumer PM at lifestyle tech where the product feels physical or cultural.**
Pinterest, Spotify (Wrapped, podcasts, audiobooks are all portfolio plays), Netflix consumer products, Duolingo. These satisfy "lo veo en la calle, en otros."

### Tier 3 (worth knowing about)

**Venture studio brand builder.** Pattern Brands, BRRD, Sandbridge Capital. They literally create new consumer brands portfolio style. Niche but exactly this description.

## How this updates the current target list

The confirmed 13 companies in [[creative-builder-strategy]] already lean this direction (CPG, retail, big tech, entertainment). Questions worth asking when next reviewing that list:

- Is the list weighted enough toward **challenger CPG with portfolios** vs the big three (Coca, Pepsi, Unilever)? Challenger brands match "ser owner, ambiente joven, jefe con strong background" much more cleanly than a 100,000 person org.
- Does the list include any **brand creation studios** (Red Antler, Pattern, Sub Rosa)? Those do not show up in standard MBA recruiting but match this description perfectly.
- **Innovation arms inside big CPG** can be a great hybrid: brand of a giant, scrappy team of a startup. Worth a separate sub list.
