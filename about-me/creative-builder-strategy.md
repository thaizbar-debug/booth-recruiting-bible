# Creative Builder Strategy — Summer 2027

**Date confirmed:** June 18, 2026.
**Supersedes:** the consulting-first Track 1 framing in CLAUDE.md. The pre-MBA consulting applications (McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Kearney, L.E.K., etc) were applied to for optionality during the pre-MBA window. Post-MBA, consulting is Plan Z.

## Positioning

"Creative builder" is the recruiting identity. The category is trending in industry (reference voices: Nik Sharma, Ana Andjelic, Web Smith, Brandon Doyle). The keyword is **builder**: people who ship tangible artifacts (products, brands, experiences), not just analyze or write about them. That is what differentiates this profile from a generic strategy MBA in the eyes of brand and product hiring leaders at consumer companies.

## Focus area

The umbrella is **consumer experience**, where brand, product, and experience converge. The role categories that live under this umbrella:

1. Brand Management at CPG
2. Product Marketing at big tech consumer
3. Design Strategy at IDEO and adjacent firms
4. DTC and retail innovation
5. Entertainment, sports, and luxury experiences

Design is one lens within this. It is not the umbrella. Do not narrow the search to "design jobs."

## Why consulting is Plan Z

Consulting was the safe-sounding post-MBA story in the MBA application essays. It is not the real target. Consulting remains optional fallback because the Booth pipeline is the strongest in the world, but actual recruiting energy goes to the 13 confirmed consumer targets below.

## Recruiting approach

### Tier 1 execution plan

1. Pick one decision maker per company (Brand Manager, Head of Strategy, VP Brand, Founder).
2. Build the artifact specific to that company (15 to 30 hours each).
3. Send cold outreach with the artifact as the hook ("I spent 20 hours analyzing [your brand]. Would love your reaction on the 3 assumptions I got wrong.").
4. Follow up at +7 days and +21 days.

**Pace:** 4 to 6 hours per week of artifact work starting September 2026. Total Year 1 commitment: 60 to 180 hours.

### Tier 2 execution plan

1. Attend Booth campus events September through November 2026.
2. Apply by published deadlines.
3. Case interview prep (consulting case prep transfers, plus brand case prep for Disney/Spotify/Pinterest-style roles).
4. Light cold outreach to Booth alumni at each company. No artifact required.

## Honest tradeoffs

1. **Artifact time bet.** 4 to 6 artifacts at 15 to 30 hours each is real load alongside classes and recruiting. This is the bet.
2. **Tier 2 is not low effort.** Disney and Spotify MBA recruiting is brutal. Tier 2 is real fallback, not coast mode.
3. **Visa constraint unchanged.** Every Tier 1 and Tier 2 company must sponsor F-1 to OPT or H-1B. Verify sponsorship per company before deep investment.
4. **Narrative inconsistency with MBA essays.** The application essays still say "join a global consulting firm with strong CPG/retail or growth and marketing practice." The new strategy skips the consulting step. Anyone reading both will notice. Acceptable for now because the essays already mention CPG/retail/marketing as the destination, just the path is different.
