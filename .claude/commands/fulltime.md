## The Full-Time Offer Hierarchy

### Path A — Return Offer Conversion (Ideal)
The best full-time offer is the one from your summer internship. This is Thaiz's primary target.

**How to maximize conversion probability during the summer:**
- [ ] Deliver the internship project with measurably strong impact — not just "completed" but "exceeded expectations"
- [ ] Build genuine relationships with senior mentors and peers — they advocate for you in review
- [ ] Ask for feedback at weeks 2, 4, and 6 — don't wait for end-of-summer review
- [ ] Be visible but not annoying — attend socials, contribute in team meetings, go beyond the project scope
- [ ] Clarify the return offer process early (Who decides? What timeline? What criteria?)
- [ ] At week 8, ask your mentor: "Is there anything you'd recommend I do differently in the final weeks?"

**For consulting internship → FT conversion:**
- Final presentation quality is disproportionately important
- Client relationship matters — build trust with the client team, not just your internal team
- Ask your engagement manager: "How can I make this final deck undeniable?"

**For CPG internship → FT conversion:**
- Show you can own a P&L decision, not just execute tasks
- Develop a clear recommendation with data — don't just present findings, advocate
- Make your cross-functional relationships visible (work with sales, finance, insights teams)

### Path B — External FT Recruiting (Parallel or Fallback)

If she's exploring beyond her internship firm, or didn't receive a return offer, this is the path.

**Timing:**
- Sept–Nov 2027: Firm info sessions, applications open
- Dec 2027–Feb 2028: Interviews, final rounds
- Mar–Apr 2028: Offers, decisions
- May/June 2028: Graduation + OPT/STEM OPT begins

**Key difference from summer recruiting:**
- Full-time roles have narrower windows and higher competition
- Booth's brand is strong but second-year positioning matters — she needs to lead with summer internship impact, not just MBA coursework
- By this point she should have a tighter narrative: "I did X at [firm] and it confirmed that [specific role/industry] is exactly where I want to build my career"

## Visa Planning for Full-Time

This is non-trivial and must be planned early.

**Timeline:**
- **OPT:** Begins at graduation (May/June 2028). Gives 12 months of work authorization.
- **STEM OPT:** If she completes any STEM-designated courses/concentration at Booth, she can extend to 36 months total.
- **H-1B:** Companies must file by April for October start. If she graduates in May 2028, the earliest H-1B would be October 2029 — meaning OPT must bridge that gap.

**Actions before FT recruiting:**
- [ ] Confirm with Booth's International Student Office which concentrations qualify for STEM OPT
- [ ] Shortlist only companies with H-1B sponsorship track records (all MBB, all major CPG companies do)
- [ ] Be prepared to address visa proactively in final-round conversations: "I'll be on OPT for the first 12–36 months and will need H-1B sponsorship — I understand this is standard practice at [firm]."

**LATAM Option:** If US visa becomes a barrier, she has exceptional positioning for LATAM leadership roles at global firms (BCG Lima/Bogotá/São Paulo, P&G LATAM HQ in Panama, Nestlé LATAM, AB InBev LATAM). This is a strength, not a fallback.
