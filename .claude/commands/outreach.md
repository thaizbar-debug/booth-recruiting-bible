## The 6-Point Email Framework (Steve Dalton)

Every cold outreach email must hit all 6 points. Under 75 words total. No exceptions.

| Point | What It Is | What It Is NOT |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Connection Lead** | Name-drop the specific mutual connection or LinkedIn alumni group that got you here | "I found you on LinkedIn" (generic) |
| 2. **About Them** | 1 sentence showing you read their profile/work — specific detail | Flattery ("You have such an impressive background") |
| 3. **Functional Focus** | Your interest area in 3–5 words | A summary of your whole resume |
| 4. **Ask for Knowledge, Not a Job** | "I'd love 20 minutes of your time and advice" | "Do you have any open roles?" |
| 5. **Peer Context** | 1 line: who you are (Booth MBA candidate, your background) | A full bio |
| 6. **LinkedIn Link** | Your LinkedIn URL at the end | Attaching your resume |

### The Template

```
Subject: Booth MBA — Quick Question for [Their Name]

Hi [First Name],

[CONNECTION LEAD: I came across your profile through the Booth alumni network / [Mutual Name] suggested I reach out / I noticed you're a fellow Booth alum at [Company].]

[ABOUT THEM: Your work on [specific project/initiative] really caught my attention — especially [one specific detail].]

I'm a Booth MBA candidate interested in [FUNCTIONAL FOCUS: brand strategy in CPG / consulting in the consumer sector / marketing strategy].

Would you have 20 minutes to share your perspective? I'm trying to understand [SPECIFIC QUESTION — what the role actually involves day-to-day / how you made the transition from X to Y / what you wish you knew before recruiting].

[PEER CONTEXT: I come from a product and consulting background in Latin America (BCP, Favo) and am focusing my recruiting on [track].]

[LinkedIn: your-linkedin-url]

Thank you,
Thaiz
```

### Quality Checklist Before Sending

- [ ] Under 75 words (body only, not subject/signature)
- [ ] Named a specific connection lead — not generic "I found you on LinkedIn"
- [ ] One specific detail about them that shows I read their profile
- [ ] Asking for advice/knowledge — NOT for a job or referral
- [ ] LinkedIn link included — NOT resume attachment
- [ ] No spelling errors, no "Dear Sir/Madam"

## The 3v7 Follow-Up Routine

After every outreach email, set two calendar reminders:

### +3 Business Days
**Action:** If no response, do NOT follow up with the same person yet.
**Instead:** Find a different contact at the same company and send a fresh 6-Point Email.
- Go back to `companies/lamp-list.md` — find a second alumni at the same firm
- Or use the LinkedIn Groups hack: search `[company] Booth` to surface more contacts

### +7 Business Days
**Action:** If still no response from the original person, send **one** follow-up.
- Same email thread (reply to your original, or new email referencing it)
- Different channel: if you emailed, try LinkedIn DM; if LinkedIn, try email
- Keep it to 2–3 sentences: "Following up on my email below — I know you're busy. Happy to connect whenever works for you."
- This is the **last attempt** for this person. Move on after this.

### The 2-Attempt Max Rule
Maximum 2 total contacts per person (original + one follow-up). After that: note "Unresponsive" in `networking/tracker.md` and move on. Don't take it personally — it's about timing.

## Referral Ask (After TIARA — Not During)

After a successful informational meeting (TIARA complete + follow-up sent), wait 1 week then send a referral ask. This goes in a **separate email** — never during the informational meeting itself.

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject] — One More Question

Hi [First Name],

Thank you again for our conversation last week — [one specific thing you took away].

I've been doing more research on [Company] and I'm increasingly confident it's where I want to focus my recruiting. 

Would you be comfortable putting my name forward for [specific role / summer associate program]? I completely understand if the timing isn't right.

Thank you,
Thaiz
[LinkedIn]
```

**Rules for the referral ask:**
- Wait at least 1 week after the TIARA conversation
- Only ask Boosters — never Obligates or Curmudgeons
- Be specific about the role you want referred for
- Give them an easy out ("I understand if the timing isn't right")
- One ask per person — don't follow up if they don't respond to this

## Response Handling

**If they respond yes to meeting:** Reply within 24 hours. Offer 3 specific times. Confirm with a calendar invite. Prep using the TIARA framework (`/informational` command).

**If they respond but can't meet:** Thank them, ask if there's anyone else at the firm they'd suggest. This often surfaces a more available contact.

**If they refer you to HR:** Politely note it; still use their name when you apply. A referral through HR is better than no referral.

**If no response after 3v7:** Note "Unresponsive" in tracker. Don't delete — they may become responsive in 6 months when recruiting heats up.
