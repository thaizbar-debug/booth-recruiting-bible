# Company Research & Industry Intelligence

Sources for discovering target companies, tracking industry trends, and building the market knowledge that makes coffee chats and interviews sharp.

---

## Retail, CPG & Consumer Industry

Use these to find companies in the retail, CPG, and consumer space — and to stay current on the industry so you can speak intelligently in interviews and coffee chats.

| Source | URL | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| Chain Store Age | https://chainstoreage.com/ | Retail industry news; find mid-size and large retailers expanding or hiring; track store openings, tech investments, strategy shifts |
| RetailWire | https://retailwire.com/ | Retail industry discussion and analysis; understand trends before interviews; find company names you hadn't considered |
| National Retail Federation | https://nrf.com/ | The trade org for retail — events, company directory, executive contacts; NRF Big Show (January) is a major networking event |
| WWD (Women's Wear Daily) | https://wwd.com/ | Fashion, beauty, luxury, and retail — key for brands like LVMH, L'Oréal, PVH, Tapestry, Capri; executive moves and company news |
| Business of Fashion | https://www.businessoffashion.com/ | Deep analysis of fashion and luxury industry; good for understanding brand strategy at LVMH, Kering, Richemont, Nike, Adidas |
| Accenture Song Index | https://www.accenture.com/us-en/about/accenture-song-index | Ranks brands by creative and marketing effectiveness — use to identify which companies are investing in brand and marketing |

**How to use for company discovery:**
- Read Chain Store Age and RetailWire weekly to spot companies growing aggressively (= hiring)
- Use NRF's company directory to find retailers you may not know
- WWD and BoF to identify fashion/luxury brand strategy roles you hadn't considered
- Accenture Song Index to rank target companies by marketing investment level

## Company Lists & Rankings

Use these lists to systematically discover companies you should target that you haven't thought of yet.

| Source | URL | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| Inc 5000 | https://www.inc.com/inc5000 | Fastest-growing private companies in the US — many are at the exact size where MBA hires have real impact |
| Forbes Top Private Companies | https://www.forbes.com/lists/top-private-companies/ | Large private companies that hire MBAs but don't appear on public company radar |
| Red Herring Top 100 | https://www.redherring.com/events/top-100/ | High-growth tech companies — BD, growth strategy, and corporate strategy roles |

**How to use:**
- Filter Inc 5000 by industry (retail, consumer goods, marketing) → find fast-growing companies you've never heard of
- Forbes Private Companies → identify large private CPGs or retailers that aren't on your radar
- Red Herring → tech companies needing MBA-level strategy or BD hires

## Tech & Startup Intelligence

Use these to stay current on tech company moves — acquisitions, funding rounds, new product launches — and to find BD/growth/strategy roles at tech companies.

| Source | URL | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| TechCrunch | https://techcrunch.com/ | Funding announcements, company launches, exec hires — a just-funded company is actively hiring |
| The Verge | https://www.theverge.com/ | Consumer tech and product strategy analysis; understand how tech companies build products and brands |
| Yahoo Finance | https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ | Financial news, earnings, M&A — understand target companies' financial health before applying |
| SmartBrief | https://www.smartbrief.com/ | Industry-specific news digests (retail, marketing, food/bev, tech) — subscribe to the 2-3 relevant to your target tracks |

**How to use:**
- Set TechCrunch alerts for "Series B", "Series C" companies in consumer/retail/marketing — those are your BD/growth targets
- Read SmartBrief daily digest for your industry (10 minutes/day keeps you interview-ready)
- Check Yahoo Finance before any coffee chat to know the company's recent performance

## PM & Product Resources

These are for building PM credibility and preparing for any PM or Technical PM roles. Also useful for understanding product thinking, which is valued in consulting and corporate strategy interviews.

| Source | URL | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| Mind the Product | https://www.mindtheproduct.com/ | PM community, articles, case studies, job board — read to build product vocabulary and frameworks |
| SVPG (Silicon Valley Product Group) | https://www.svpg.com/ | Marty Cagan's blog — the gold standard for PM thinking; read before any PM interview |
| Atlassian PM Guide | https://www.atlassian.com/blog/agile/product-managers-guide-release-planning | Agile and release planning frameworks — useful for Technical PM roles and for demonstrating PM fluency |
| Cracking the PM Interview | https://www.crackingthepminterview.com/ | The book's companion site — interview frameworks, sample questions, prep guides |
| The PM Interview | https://thepminterview.com/ | Practice PM interview questions with sample answers; use before any PM or product-adjacent interview |
| Break Into Tech | https://www.breakinto.tech/ | Career transition resources for getting into tech — useful for PM and product strategy roles at tech companies |

**How to use:**
- Read SVPG weekly if pursuing any PM or product strategy role
- Mind the Product for frameworks and vocabulary to drop naturally in interviews
- Cracking the PM Interview + The PM Interview for active interview prep (not passive reading)
- Break Into Tech for understanding tech company hiring processes as an MBA
