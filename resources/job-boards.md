# Job Boards Master List

All job boards to search when looking for opportunities. Organized by primary relevance to Thaiz's target tracks.

## How to Use

When running `/job-search`, check boards in priority order by track. Don't spray-and-pray — pick the 2-3 most relevant boards per search session and go deep on each one before moving to the next.

## Search Strategy by Track

### Running a consulting search session
1. MBA Global Net → filter by "MBA" + "consulting" + "strategy"
2. The Ladders → "$100K+" + "management consulting" or "corporate strategy"
3. Exec Appointments → "strategy associate" or "corporate strategy manager"

### Running a CPG/Brand search session
1. CPG Jobs → "brand manager" + "associate" or "MBA"
2. AMA → "brand strategy" or "brand management" + "MBA"
3. Marketing Jobs → "brand manager" or "product marketing manager"

### Running a BD & Growth search session
1. Crunchboard → "business development" + "strategy" or "growth strategy"
2. VentureBeat Jobs → "business development" + "MBA" or "strategy"
3. The Ladders → "business development manager" or "growth strategy"

### Running a Consumer Insights search session
1. MR Web USA → "consumer insights" or "market research" + "MBA"
2. AMA → "consumer insights" or "brand analytics"
3. CPG Jobs → "shopper insights" or "category management"

## LinkedIn (Primary)

LinkedIn remains the primary job search platform. All boards above are supplementary — use them to find roles that may not appear on LinkedIn, or to discover companies you hadn't considered.

**LinkedIn search strategy for Thaiz:**
- Filter: MBA Jobs, Chicago (or remote/US), posted last 30 days
- Keywords by track: "MBA consulting associate", "brand manager MBA", "corporate strategy MBA", "business development MBA", "growth strategy associate"
- Follow target companies to get job alerts instantly
- Search for Booth alumni in target roles → reach out BEFORE applying
