# Roles Explained: Brand Manager vs Business Development vs Growth

**Date captured:** June 25, 2026.
**Purpose:** Reference doc for understanding three role types that often get confused. Use when filtering job postings, prepping for interviews, or explaining your target role to others (alumni, recruiters, friends).

## Brand Manager (BM) in CPG

**What it is:** "Mini CEO" of a brand, sub brand, or product line. At Magic Spoon, you could be BM of "Cocoa flavor" or BM of "the entire cereal line" depending on seniority.

**Responsible for:**
- **Brand P&L:** revenue, market share, margin, marketing spend ROI
- **Positioning:** what the brand says, who it talks to, what the consumer feels
- **Product:** decisions on new flavors, sizes, formats (innovation pipeline)
- **Campaigns:** briefing creative and media agencies, approving final creative
- **Pricing and packaging:** shelf price decisions, packaging redesigns
- **Distribution:** prioritizing channels (Whole Foods vs Target vs DTC)

**Typical day:**
- Reviews Nielsen / Circana / IRI data (market share, sell through)
- Briefs creative agency on a new campaign
- Presents annual plan to leadership
- Meets with R&D on the next flavor to launch
- Analyzes competitor activity

**Career path:** ABM (Associate Brand Manager) → BM → Senior BM → Marketing Director → VP Marketing → CMO.

**Fit with Thaiz's 6 patterns:** 6 of 6. This is the position described almost word for word.

## Business Development (BD)

**What it is:** Revenue generation through **external deals**, partnerships, strategic alliances, channel expansion. NOT the same as Brand Management.

**Responsible for:**
- **Deal pipeline:** identifying the next partner, distributor, retailer, licensor
- **Negotiating terms:** commercial structure, revenue share, exclusivity
- **Expansion:** entering a new country, opening a new channel, signing a big retailer
- **M&A or licensing** in some companies

**Typical day:**
- Calls with potential partners
- Structures a term sheet with legal
- Models revenue from a new deal
- Builds relationships with buyers at Target, Whole Foods, Costco
- Pitches the CFO on why this deal is worth pursuing

**Key difference from BM:**
- BM builds the brand **internally** (what it is, what it says, what it sells)
- BD builds distribution and partnerships **externally** (where it sells, with whom)

BD tends to be more transactional, more "deal making," less "creative builder." Not a natural match for the 6 patterns (low "tangible in the wild," low "concept architect," more relational and commercial).

## Growth (mostly in tech)

**What it is:** Optimization of the user acquisition, activation, retention, and monetization funnel. Very data heavy. Almost always in digital products.

**Responsible for:**
- **User metrics:** CAC (cost of acquisition), LTV (lifetime value), conversion rate, retention curve, viral coefficient
- **Experimentation:** A/B test roadmap to improve the funnel
- **Channels:** mix of paid (Meta ads, Google ads), organic, referral, digital partnerships
- **Onboarding and activation:** the user's first 7 days in the product
- **Pricing tests, paywalls, subscription optimization**

**Typical day:**
- Reviews Mixpanel / Amplitude dashboards
- Designs the next experiment (ex. "what happens if we change the CTA copy?")
- Meets with engineering to ship the experiment
- Statistical analysis of results
- Meets with paid media team on campaign performance

**Key difference from BM:**
- BM works with **brand, emotion, positioning, perception**, cycles of 6 to 24 months
- Growth works with **funnels, metrics, experiments, data**, cycles of 2 to 4 weeks
- BM asks "what does the consumer feel when they see my brand?" Growth asks "what percentage converted this week?"

**Fit with Thaiz's 6 patterns:** partial. Has the "concept architect" quality at Head of Growth level, but lacks "tangible in the wild" and "emotional resonance." Almost always software, almost never physical product.

## Comparison table

| Dimension | Brand Manager | Business Dev | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owns | Brand + product P&L | Deals + partnerships | User metrics |
| Typical industry | CPG, beauty, fashion | Any, lots of B2B | Tech, SaaS, DTC |
| Type of work | Creative + strategic | Relational + commercial | Analytical + experimental |
| Tangible output | Product on shelf, campaign | Signed contract | Metric improvement |
| Feedback cycle | 6 to 24 months | 3 to 12 months | 1 to 4 weeks |
| Thaiz's fit | 6 of 6 patterns | 2 of 6 patterns | 3 of 6 patterns |

## Verdict

**Brand Manager is the position Thaiz described, almost word for word.** "Being owner, charting the path, seeing the product in the streets, emotional connection, portfolio of variants" equals brand management in CPG.

Business Development and Growth can be interesting at specific moments in a career (BD to learn deal making, Growth to break into a digital scale up), but neither is what was described.
