# Resume Storage

One folder per role track. Each version is numbered (v1, v2...) so nothing is ever overwritten.

## Folder Structure

```
resumes/
├── consulting/          ← Management consulting (MBB, Tier 2)
├── corporate-strategy/  ← Internal strategy, corporate development
├── growth-strategy/     ← Revenue growth, BD, growth strategy roles
├── marketing/           ← Brand/product/customer marketing at CPG
├── product-marketing/   ← Tech-adjacent product marketing roles
└── business-development/← BD, partnerships, commercial roles
```

## Versioning Rules

- Never overwrite an existing file — always increment the version number
- After each new version, add a row to the Version Log above
- Note what changed so you can go back to any version for any reason
- Keep the same base filename per track: `CV_Thaiz_Barthelmess_[Track]_v[N].pdf`

## Resume Tailoring Notes by Track

### Consulting
- Lead with strategic impact and structured problem solving
- Emphasize: stakeholder management, frameworks, C-level exposure, cross-industry breadth
- Strongest bullets: Apoyo projects (EBITDA targets, volume growth, board presentations)
- Tone: Rigorous, analytical, outcome-driven

### Corporate Strategy
- Lead with internal transformation and long-term planning
- Emphasize: 5-year strategic plans, org design, governance, financial modeling
- Strongest bullets: Apoyo org redesign for university, BCP digital wallet growth
- Tone: Visionary + execution-oriented

### Growth Strategy / Business Development
- Lead with revenue generation and commercial metrics
- Emphasize: CAC reduction ($80→$5), retention (80%), margin expansion (2%→10%), asset growth ($15M→$25M)
- Strongest bullets: Favo B2B model, BCP digital wallet
- Tone: Commercial, metrics-first

### Product Marketing
- Lead with customer insight and product launch success
- Emphasize: iOS/Android app launch, Live Shopping (112% orders), SDK integration
- Strongest bullets: Favo native app launch, BCP SDK
- Tone: User-centric, cross-functional

### Marketing (CPG/Brand)
- Lead with customer understanding and brand-building instincts
- Emphasize: GTM strategy (fishmeal line), LATAM market expertise, consumer segmentation
- Strongest bullets: Fishmeal GTM, Favo catalog/pricing strategy
- Tone: Consumer-obsessed, strategic
